Motivation: 测量D介子衰变中的相干因子和强相位差对于精确确定CKM幺正三角形角γ至关重要。这些参数是LHCb和Belle II实验测量γ角的关键输入，而γ角的精确测量对检验标准模型和寻找新物理具有重要意义。

Method: 利用BESIII实验在质心能量3.773 GeV处收集的7.93 fb⁻¹数据，通过量子关联的D\bar{D}对产生。分析采用标记-信号方法：一个D介子重建为信号衰变模式（D→K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻或D→K⁻π⁺π⁰），另一个D介子重建为标记模式。对于D→K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻衰变，还进一步在四个相空间区间内进行了参数测量。

Result: 测得相干因子：R_{K3π}=0.51±0.04，R_{Kππ⁰}=0.75±0.03；强相位差：δ_D^{K3π}=(182^{+14}_{-13})°，δ_D^{Kππ⁰}=(209^{+7}_{-8})°。对于D→K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻衰变，在四个相空间区间内获得了比先前BESIII结果更高的精度。预计基于这些参数，未来γ测量的不确定性可降至约3.5°。

Conclusion: 该研究显著提高了D介子衰变中相干因子和强相位差的测量精度，为LHCb和Belle II实验精确测量CKM角γ提供了关键输入。特别是D→K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻衰变参数的精化，有望将未来γ测量的不确定性降至约3.5°，这对检验标准模型和探索新物理具有重要意义。

Abstract: Improved measurements of the coherence factors and strong-phase differences in $D\to K^-π^+π^+π^-$ and $D\to K^-π^+π^0$ decays are reported, using quantum-correlated $D\bar{D}$ pairs produced in $e^+e^-$ annihilation at a center-of-mass energy of $3.773\,\mathrm{GeV}$, where $D$ denotes a quantum superposition of the flavour-specific $D^{0}$ and $\bar{D}^{0}$ mesons. The analysis employs a dataset collected by the BESIII experiment, corresponding to an integrated luminosity of $7.93~\rm fb^{-1}$. The observables sensitive to the coherence factors and strong-phase differences are measured by reconstructing one $D$ meson in the signal mode and the other in a tag mode. These parameters provide essential inputs to the measurement of the angle $γ$ of the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa Unitarity Triangle in the LHCb and Belle II experiments. The coherence factors are determined to be $R_{K3π}=0.51\pm0.04$ and $R_{Kππ^0}=0.75\pm0.03$, and the strong-phase differences are $δ_D^{K3π}=\left(182^{+14}_{-13}\right)^\circ$ and $δ_D^{Kππ^0}=\left(209^{+7}_{-8}\right)^\circ$, where the uncertainties include both statistical and systematic contributions. For $D\to K^-π^+π^+π^-$, the parameters have been further determined in four phase-space bins with improved precision compared to the previous BESIII results. The uncertainty on future $γ$ measurements from the knowledge of $D\to K^-π^+π^+π^-$ parameters is expected to be reduced to approximately 3.5$^\circ$.
