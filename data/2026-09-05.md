### [1] [Hadronic Mono-Z Dark Matter Sensitivity with Flow Matching on CMS Open Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02923)
*Hitesh Rasineni,Bhavishya Chebrolu*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文利用CMS Run2015D HTMHT开放数据（对应2.26 fb^-1）开展了强子单Z玻色子暗物质产生的预期灵敏度研究。作者采用条件流匹配连续归一化流（CFM-CNF）对选定事件进行背景建模，并引入哨兵值填补、训练/验证集索引持久化及最小背景产量阈值（20事件）等策略来缓解伪影和样本内评分偏差。基线分析对三个简化模型基准给出2.89σ、7.62σ和7.41σ的预期显著性；消融实验显示移除额外喷注运动学细节会使显著性降低53-71%，表明额外喷注拓扑结构在强子单Z通道中具有显著区分能力。本研究为预期灵敏度而非非盲分析。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 针对强子单Z暗物质产生通道，传统搜寻方法主要依赖单喷注特征，而论文动机在于检验额外喷注拓扑结构能否提供额外区分能力，并探索利用CMS开放数据进行暗物质搜寻的可行性。

Method: 利用CMS Run2015D HTMHT开放数据挑选强子单Z事件（1439523例），以条件流匹配连续归一化流（CFM-CNF）对HTMHT选中事件建模背景并在验证集上评分。为抑制缺失对象特征导致的伪影与避免样本内评分偏差，采用哨兵值填补未定义角特征、持久化训练/验证集划分索引，并设定最小背景产量20事件的working point选取约束；对模拟信号应用基于触发器的离线触发代理。另进行消融实验移除额外喷注运动学特征以量化其贡献。

Result: 基线分析对三个简化模型基准分别给出2.89σ、7.62σ和7.41σ的预期显著性。消融实验中，去除额外喷注详细运动学信息后预期显著性下降53-71%，证明额外喷注拓扑携带大量判别信息。

Conclusion: 强子单Z通道中额外喷注的拓扑结构对暗物质搜寻灵敏度贡献显著；基于条件流匹配生成模型的开放数据背景建模方法可行，但该结果仅为预期灵敏度（未进行非盲/unblinding），需在局限性与可复现性讨论中审视其可靠性。

Abstract: We present a projected sensitivity study for hadronic mono-$Z$ dark-matter production using CMS Run~2015D HTMHT open data corresponding to 2.256382381~\invfb, from which 1{,}439{,}523 events satisfy the hadronic mono-$Z$ selection. Backgrounds are modelled with a conditional flow-matching continuous normalizing flow trained on the selected HTMHT events and evaluated on a held-out validation split reweighted to the full selected population. To mitigate artifacts from missing-object features and avoid in-sample scoring bias we apply sentinel imputation for undefined angular features, persist the train/validation split indices, and enforce a minimum reported background yield of 20 events when selecting the working point. A signal-side offline trigger proxy is applied to the simulated signal before scoring. Under this procedure the baseline analysis yields expected significances of 2.89$σ$, 7.62$σ$, and 7.41$σ$ for three simplified-model benchmarks. An ablation study that removes the detailed extra-jet kinematics reduces the expected significance by 53--71\%, indicating that extra-jet topology carries substantial discriminating power in the hadronic mono-$Z$ channel. These results are projected sensitivities (no unblinding performed); the limitations and reproducibility of the study are discussed in Sections limitations and reproducibility.
