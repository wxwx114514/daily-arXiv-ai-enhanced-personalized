### [3] [Search for resonant and nonresonant production of pairs of dijet resonances with b jets in the final state in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21850)
*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: CMS利用136 fb⁻¹的13 TeV质子-质子对撞数据，首次搜索成对产生的、衰变为b夸克和轻味夸克的全分辨双喷注共振态，给出四喷注与双喷注共振态产生的上限，并排除质量0.5–0.8 TeV的超对称顶夸克对产生及2–7 TeV的较重双夸克质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有LHC搜索尚未覆盖"成对产生的、两个共振态均可全分辨、且末态含b夸克"的双喷注共振态情形，此类非标准模型态可能源于R宇称破缺的超对称或双夸克等新物理过程，需要专门搜索以拓展探测灵敏度。

Method: 使用CMS探测器在√s=13 TeV质子-质子对撞中采集的、积分亮度为136 fb⁻¹的数据样本，搜索成对产生且质量相同的双喷注共振态，每个共振态衰变为一个b夸克和一个轻味夸克。分析分别针对非共振产生和经由中间共振态的四喷注产生两种情形，并对末态含b喷注的四喷注与双喷注共振态产生设置上限。

Abstract: A search is presented for pair-produced dijet resonances of identical mass, each decaying into a bottom (b) quark and a light-flavor quark. The search uses a data sample corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 136 fb$^{-1}$ collected with the CMS detector in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV. Results are presented separately for nonresonant production and for the case where the four-jet production proceeds via an intermediate resonant state. Upper limits are reported on the production of four-jet and dijet resonances with b jets in the final state. This is the first LHC search for resonant production of pairs of fully resolved dijet resonances with b jets in the final state, in which all resonances considered correspond to non-standard-model states. For nonresonant production, the most significant excess occurs at an average dijet mass of 1.0 TeV, with a local significance of 2.1 standard deviations. Additionally, the results exclude pair production of top squarks with masses between 0.5 and 0.8 TeV, extending previous searches for $R$-parity-violating decays to strange and b quarks. In the search for resonant production, the most significant excess occurs at a four-jet resonance mass of 3.1 TeV and a dijet resonance mass of 1.3 TeV, with a local significance of 2.6 standard deviations. The results are also used to set limits at 95% confidence level on the diquark-to-diquarks model and exclude the heavier diquark masses between 2 and 7 TeV.
