### [1] [Measurement of the Higgs boson decay to a low-mass dilepton system and a photon in $pp$ collisions at $\sqrt{s} =$ 13 and 13.6 TeV with the ATLAS detector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03369)
*ATLAS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 本文测量了希格斯玻色子衰变到一个光子和一对轻子（电子或缪子）的过程，其中轻子对不变质量m_ll < 30 GeV。利用ATLAS探测器在LHC上收集的164 fb^-1数据（13.6 TeV，2022-2024年），测得信号强度μ = 0.64^{+0.44}_{-0.39}，显著度为1.7σ。与2015-2018年139 fb^-1的13 TeV数据组合后，信号强度为μ = 1.03^{+0.35}_{-0.32}，显著度达到3.4σ。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Higgs玻色子衰变为光子和轻子对（H→ℓℓγ）是一个稀有过程，其中轻子对质量低于30 GeV的贡献来自Z玻色子之外的低质量区间（包括真实光子辐射和可能的超出标准模型的贡献）。该测量旨在检验标准模型的预言，并搜索可能的新物理信号。

Method: 使用ATLAS探测器在LHC上收集的两组数据：(1) 2022-2024年13.6 TeV质子-质子对撞数据，积分亮度164 fb^-1；(2) 2015-2018年13 TeV数据，积分亮度139 fb^-1。测量H→ℓℓγ过程（ℓ = e或μ），要求m_ll < 30 GeV，通过拟合信号强度参数μ（观测信号产额与标准模型预期之比）来提取结果，并将两组数据的结果进行组合。

Result: 新数据（13.6 TeV）的信号强度为μ = 0.64^{+0.44}_{-0.39}（预期μ = 1.00^{+0.47}_{-0.41}），观测（预期）显著度为1.7σ（2.5σ）。与旧数据组合后，信号强度为μ = 1.03^{+0.35}_{-0.32}（预期μ = 1.00^{+0.34}_{-0.32}），观测（预期）显著度达到3.4σ（3.3σ）。

Conclusion: 新的13.6 TeV数据单独观测到1.7σ的超出信号，与标准模型预期相符但尚无定论；与13 TeV数据组合后，观测显著度提升至3.4σ，接近5σ的"发现"标准，为H→ℓℓγ（低质量轻子对）过程提供了进一步支持标准模型的证据，但尚未达到决定性发现水平。

Abstract: A measurement is presented for the Higgs boson decaying into a photon and a pair of electrons or muons with an invariant mass $m_{\ell\ell} <$ 30 GeV, using $pp$ collision data at $\sqrt{s} =$ 13.6 TeV recorded by the ATLAS detector at the Large Hadron Collider during the years 2022-2024, corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 164 fb$^{-1}$. The best-fit value of the signal strength parameter, defined as the ratio of the observed signal yield to the one expected in the Standard Model, is measured to be $μ= 0.64^{+0.44}_{-0.39}$ compared with the expected value of $μ= 1.00^{+0.47}_{-0.41}$. The corresponding observed (expected) signal significance is 1.7 (2.5) standard deviations under the background-only hypothesis. The result is combined with a similar search performed using 139 fb$^{-1}$ of $pp$ collision data at $\sqrt{s} =$ 13 TeV collected during the years 2015-2018. This combination gives an observed (expected) signal strength of $μ= 1.03^{+0.35}_{-0.32}\,(μ= 1.00^{+0.34}_{-0.32})$, corresponding to an observed (expected) significance of 3.4 (3.3) standard deviations.

TL;DR: 该论文利用CMD-3探测器在VEPP-2000对撞机上搜索e+e-→f1(1285)反应。在质心能量1.20-1.36 GeV的扫描数据（109.1 pb^-1）和共振峰能量1282 MeV处的专用数据（51.7 pb^-1）中，在e+e-→ηπ0π0通道找到26.5±7.4个事件，提供了4.5σ的C偶共振态通过双光子相互作用产生的证据，测得产生截面为0.074±0.021±0.010 nb，对应的f1(1285)→e+e-分支比为(8.3±2.3±1.1)×10^-9。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索C偶性共振态f1(1285)在e+e-对撞中的产生机制。f1(1285)是C偶性的，通常在e+e-对撞中通过单光子湮灭无法直接产生，但可以通过双光子相互作用过程产生，因此对该反应的研究有助于理解低能QCD中轴向矢量介子的性质以及光子-介子耦合机制。

Conclusion: 研究提供了4.5σ的证据，表明C偶性共振态f1(1285)可通过双光子相互作用在e+e-对撞中产生，这是对该罕见衰变道和产生机制的首次观测证据，为理解轴向矢量介子的电磁性质提供了重要实验数据。

Abstract: A search for the $e^+ e^-\to f_1(1285)$ reaction is performed with the CMD-3 detector at the VEPP-2000 collider. Data from a scan in the center-of-mass energy range Ec.m.=1.20--1.36 GeV with 109.1 pb$^{-1}$ of the integrated luminosity is used. A dedicated 51.7 pb$^{-1}$ have been collected at the $f_1(1285)$ resonance peak energy Ec.m.=1282 MeV, where $26.5 \pm 7.4$ events have been found in the $e^+ e^-\toηπ^0π^0$ reaction. No indication of the signal is observed in the scan data. We interpret the result as 4.5$σ$ evidence of the C-even resonance production in the $e^+ e^-\to f_1(1285)\toηπ^0π^0$ reaction via the two-photon interaction. The production cross section is found to be $σ(e^+ e^-\to f_1) = 0.074 \pm 0.021 \pm 0.010$ nb, and the corresponding branching fraction to the $e^+ e^-$ pair is $B(f_1(1285)\to e^+e^-) = (8.3 \pm 2.3 \pm 1.1)\times 10^{-9}$.
