### [2] [Physical Counterexamples to the Wigner Shannon Entropy Conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13312)
*Zixuan He*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 构造了Wigner函数处处为正但Shannon熵低于真空值1+lnπ的物理量子态，给出有限能量反例、Fock支集族熵展开、真空-三光子对数渐近，并证明最大熵亏损的精确标度E^γ/[ln(1/E)]^β。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检验Wigner正性与熵下界之间是否存在普适关系，尤其是Wigner非负态能否具有低于真空的Shannon熵，并刻画最小可达熵亏损的精确能量标度。

Method: 构造显式有限能量态与秩二有限Fock支集族；对固定Fock能级n和相干比例λ作熵差的t^2、t^4及t^6微扰展开；对真空-三光子构造求Wigner非负所需最小热混合权重的对数渐近；对平均光子数≤E的单模Wigner非负态作全局优化。

Conclusion: Wigner正性并不蕴含Shannon熵高于真空值；真空熵在E→0时虽被恢复，但最优熵亏损的衰减远慢于任何关于平均能量的线性普适修正。

Abstract: We construct physical quantum states with everywhere positive Wigner functions whose Shannon entropy lies below the vacuum value $1+\lnπ$. Besides an explicit finite-energy counterexample, we obtain rank-two finite-Fock-support families with analytic positivity and entropy bounds. For fixed Fock level $n$ and coherence fraction $0\leλ<1$, the entropy difference satisfies $h(W)-(1+\lnπ)=(2n-2^nλ^2)t^2+O_{n,λ}(t^4)$, yielding finite-support counterexamples for every $n\ge3$ above an explicit coherence threshold. Finally, optimizing over all one-mode Wigner-nonnegative states with mean photon number at most $E$, we prove that the maximal entropy deficit has the sharp scale $E^γ/[\ln(1/E)]^β$, where $γ\simeq0.7412033679$ and $β\simeq0.5861054961$. Thus the vacuum entropy is recovered as $E\to0$, but the optimal deficit decays much more slowly than any universal linear correction in the mean energy.

Conclusion: 增益源于架构本身——时间结构化编码在折叠吸引子上产生的有限样本归纳偏置效应，不附带计算分离性主张。作者称这是首个将量子核优势机制性定位到经典系统特定动力学区间的工作，其中关键难点为折叠分支歧义，欧氏核难以判别轨迹将归属哪一叶瓣。

Abstract: Quantum kernels promise exponentially large feature spaces, but expressive circuits render their Gram matrices uninformative, bandwidth tuning collapses them toward classical RBF, and the practical consensus is that quantum kernels add nothing on classical data. We answer another productive question, an architectural one: whether the structure of an encoding circuit can carry an inductive bias that tuned classical kernels lack. We introduce AeRot, a quantum kernel fusing amplitude encoding of l2-normalized delay windows with a grouped single-qubit rotation layer that assigns contiguous temporal blocks to each qubit, making the circuit delay-window-aware. The difficulty is fold-branch ambiguity: trajectories approaching the saddle are locally diverging, and Euclidean kernels struggle to resolve which lobe the trajectory will commit to. Structural diagnostics confirm Gram matrices structurally distinct from tuned RBF, with strictly higher target-kernel alignment at every horizon. The gain is architectural: a finite-sample inductive-bias effect of temporally structured encoding on a folded attractor, with no computational-separation claim attached.
