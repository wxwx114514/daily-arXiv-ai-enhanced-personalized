### [1] [PhyGHT: Physics-Guided HyperGraph Transformer for Signal Purification at the HL-LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20475)
*Mohammed Rakib,Luke Vaughan,Shivang Patel,Flera Rizatdinova,Alexander Khanov,Atriya Sen*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: PhyGHT是一种结合局部图注意力和全局自注意力的混合架构，通过可解释的堆积抑制门过滤软噪声，在极端堆积条件下提升顶夸克对产生信号的重建精度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高亮度大型强子对撞机（HL-LHC）将产生前所未有的数据集，但面临从约200个同时堆积碰撞的压倒性背景中提取微小信号分数的重大挑战，极端噪声严重扭曲了精确重建所需的物理可观测量。

Method: 提出物理引导超图变换器（PhyGHT），结合距离感知的局部图注意力和全局自注意力来镜像质子-质子碰撞中形成的粒子簇射物理拓扑。关键创新是集成堆积抑制门（PSG），这是一种可解释的物理约束机制，在超图聚合前明确学习过滤软噪声。

Result: PhyGHT在预测信号能量和质量校正因子方面优于ATLAS和CMS实验的最先进基线方法。通过准确重建顶夸克的不变质量，展示了机器学习创新如何直接推进科学发现。

Abstract: The High-Luminosity Large Hadron Collider (HL-LHC) at CERN will produce unprecedented datasets capable of revealing fundamental properties of the universe. However, realizing its discovery potential faces a significant challenge: extracting small signal fractions from overwhelming backgrounds dominated by approximately 200 simultaneous pileup collisions. This extreme noise severely distorts the physical observables required for accurate reconstruction. To address this, we introduce the Physics-Guided Hypergraph Transformer (PhyGHT), a hybrid architecture that combines distance-aware local graph attention with global self-attention to mirror the physical topology of particle showers formed in proton-proton collisions. Crucially, we integrate a Pileup Suppression Gate (PSG), an interpretable, physics-constrained mechanism that explicitly learns to filter soft noise prior to hypergraph aggregation. To validate our approach, we release a novel simulated dataset of top-quark pair production to model extreme pileup conditions. PhyGHT outperforms state-of-the-art baselines from the ATLAS and CMS experiments in predicting the signal's energy and mass correction factors. By accurately reconstructing the top quark's invariant mass, we demonstrate how machine learning innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration can directly advance scientific discovery at the frontiers of experimental physics and enhance the HL-LHC's discovery potential. The dataset and code are available at https://github.com/rAIson-Lab/PhyGHT

### [3] [Search for pair production of heavy resonances in final states with a photon and large-radius jets in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20477)
*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: CMS实验在13 TeV质子-质子对撞中搜索重自旋-1/2或自旋-3/2共振态t*对产生，分析ttγg末态，未发现显著信号，设定了质量下限限制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 寻找超出标准模型的新物理，特别是重共振态t*（自旋-1/2或自旋-3/2）的对产生，通过ttγg衰变通道进行探测，这是首次在该通道中搜索重共振态。

Method: 使用CMS探测器在2016-2018年收集的138 fb⁻¹数据，选择t*→tγ和t*→tg衰变的全强子末态，利用喷注子结构技术重建top夸克，通过光子和高能top夸克候选喷注重建t*候选质量。

Result: 未发现与仅背景假设的显著偏差，在95%置信水平下设定信号截面上限：自旋-1/2 t*质量低于930 GeV（观测/预期均为930 GeV），自旋-3/2 t*质量低于1330 GeV（观测）和1390 GeV（预期）被排除。

Conclusion: 这是首次在ttγg通道中搜索重共振态，利用高能光子有效压低背景，尽管t*→tγ分支比较小，但对自旋-1/2 t*的灵敏度与ttγg搜索相当，为未来新物理搜索提供了新途径。

Abstract: A search for the pair production of heavy spin-1/2 or spin-3/2 resonances (t$^*$) in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV is presented. Data collected with the CMS detector at the CERN LHC from 2016 to 2018 corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 138 fb$^{-1}$ are used. The analysis targets benchmark signal scenarios where one t$^*$ decays into a top quark (t) and a photon ($γ$), and the other into a t quark and a gluon (g), i.e., pp $\to$ t$^*\bar{\mathrm{t}}^*$ $\to$ tt$γ$g. All-hadronic final states from the t pair decay chain are selected using jet substructure techniques. The signal is probed as a function of the t$^*$ candidate mass, which is reconstructed using the photon and a top quark candidate jet. No significant deviation from the background-only hypothesis is found. Observed (expected) upper limits on the signal cross section at 95% confidence level are set, excluding masses of spin-1/2 t$^*$ particles below 930 (930) GeV and spin-3/2 t$^*$ particles below 1330 (1390) GeV. This analysis marks the first search for heavy resonances in the $\mathrm{t\bar{t}}γ$g channel. Exploiting the high-energy photon to reduce the backgrounds, this search achieves sensitivity competitive with pp $\to$ t$^*\mathrm{\bar{t}}^*$ $\to$ $\mathrm{t\bar{t}}γ$g searches for spin-1/2 t$^*$ despite the small expected t$^*$ $\to$ t$γ$ branching fraction.
