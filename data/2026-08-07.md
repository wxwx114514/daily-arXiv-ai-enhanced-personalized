### [1] [Search for resonant production of lepton-enriched semivisible jets in proton-proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05323)
*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: 该论文利用CMS探测器在CERN LHC收集的138 fb^-1质子-质子对撞数据，搜索了来源于强耦合暗扇区的轻子富集半可见喷注（SVJ）的共振产生。研究中采用双机器学习策略（图神经网络用于喷注识别，全连接神经网络用于喷注与事件级信息结合），在95%置信水平下，SVJℓ信号排除了Z'中介子质量至4.7 TeV，SVJτ信号排除了1.8至3.5 TeV质量区间，首次提供了对轻子富集半可见喷注的实验约束。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 寻找强耦合暗扇区产生的新物理信号，尤其是轻子富集的半可见喷注（SVJ），这类信号此前未被实验探索过。通过搜索具有非孤立轻子的喷注与缺失横动量对齐的终态，首次对这类信号设置实验约束。

Method: 利用CMS探测器13 TeV对撞数据（138 fb^-1），分析两类签名：全轻子味富集的SVJℓ和以τ轻子为主的SVJτ。采用双机器学习策略：图神经网络（GNN）用于喷注识别，全连接神经网络整合喷注级和事件级信息，以提升信号敏感度和背景估计。信号模型假设与标准模型夸克耦合强度为0.25的重Z'中介子，暗强子产生瞬发衰变。

Result: 在95%置信水平下，SVJℓ场景中排除了Z'中介子质量至4.7 TeV；SVJτ场景中排除了1.8至3.5 TeV的质量区间。这些结果为轻子富集半可见喷注提供了首个实验约束。

Conclusion: 该研究成功利用机器学习驱动的事件分类技术，首次为轻子富集半可见喷注信号提供了实验排除界限，覆盖了从数TeV到接近5 TeV的广泛质量范围，为后续暗扇区研究奠定了实验基础。

Abstract: This search targets the resonant production of lepton-enriched semivisible jets (SVJs) from a strongly coupled dark sector, using 138 fb$^{-1}$ of proton-proton collision data collected with the CMS detector at the CERN LHC at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV. Two scenarios are investigated: jets enriched in all lepton flavors (SVJ$\ell$ signature) and jets predominantly enriched in tau leptons (SVJ$τ$ signature). The analysis focuses on final states in which the missing transverse momentum is aligned with jets containing nonisolated leptons. A dual machine-learning strategy is employed, using a graph neural network for jet identification and a fully connected neural network that combines jet- and event-level information to enhance signal sensitivity and background estimation. The signal models assume a heavy Z' mediator with a benchmark coupling of 0.25 to standard model quarks, together with prompt decays of unstable dark hadrons. In the SVJ$\ell$ scenario, mediator masses up to 4.7 TeV are excluded at 95% confidence level, while masses between 1.8 and 3.5 TeV are excluded in the SVJ$τ$ scenario. These results provide the first experimental constraints on lepton-enriched semivisible jets.
