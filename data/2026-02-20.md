TL;DR: LUX-ZEPLIN探测器在桑福德地下研究设施测量了宇宙线μ子通量，结果为(5.09±0.08±0.10)×10⁻⁹ cm⁻²s⁻¹，这是评估暗物质、中微子等实验背景的重要数据。

Motivation: 高能宇宙线μ子到达深地下实验室时，会在探测器中产生与暗物质粒子、中微子或稀有衰变相似的信号事件。了解μ子通量和能谱对于评估由μ子及其次级粒子引起的背景率至关重要。

Method: 使用LUX-ZEPLIN探测器在桑福德地下研究设施的Davis Campus进行测量。采用366.4天的曝光时间，通过内层氙探测器（能量阈值20 MeV）和外层液体闪烁体探测器（能量阈值8 MeV）测量μ子率。

Result: 测得探测器中的μ子率为10.94±0.17 day⁻¹，对应Davis Cavern中的μ子通量为(5.09±0.08_stat.±0.10_sys.)×10⁻⁹ cm⁻²s⁻¹。

Conclusion: 该测量为评估地下实验中μ子引起的背景提供了重要数据，有助于提高暗物质、中微子等稀有事件探测实验的精度和可靠性。

Abstract: High-energy cosmic-ray muons reaching deep underground laboratories can cause events in detectors that mimic signals expected from dark matter particles, neutrinos, or rare decays. Knowledge of the muon flux and energy spectrum is important for evaluating the background rate caused by muons and their secondaries. In this paper, we report the measurement of the cosmic-ray muon flux in the Davis Campus of the Sanford Underground Research Facility with the LUX-ZEPLIN detector. Using 366.4~days of exposure, the muon rate through the detector was measured as $10.94\pm0.17_\textrm{stat.}~\textrm{day}^{-1}$ with energy thresholds of 20~MeV in the inner xenon detector and 8 MeV in the outer liquid scintillator detector. This rate corresponds to a muon flux of $(5.09\pm0.08_\textrm{stat.}\pm0.10_\textrm{sys.})\times10^{-9}~\textrm{cm}^{-2}\textrm{s}^{-1}$ in the Davis Cavern.

We employ different ansatzes: the hardware-efficient EfficientSU2 from Qiskit, the physics-inspired Hamiltonian Variational Ansatz (HVA) and HVA with symmetry breaking, and benchmark their performance using energy variance, entanglement entropy, spin correlations, and magnetization.
